What we help with

Most of our work falls into four areas. Each one starts with a problem we hear about often.

Renewing Organizations

Your organization has outgrown the way it's set up.

Expectations keep rising, revenue hasn't kept pace, governance requirements are expanding, and your community is drifting away. For organizations that started as a successful project, that success can begin to feel like a threat.

How we can help

Governance
We work with your board or advisory group on structure, roles, responsibilities and practices, so people stay engaged and decisions are transparent and accountable.
Staffing and structure
We look at what your organization needs to run well, then build succession, recruitment, retention and staff development plans to get there.
Community engagement
We help you re-engage stakeholders, volunteers and supporters through a community-led approach to working groups, sprints, conferences and communications such as your annual report.
Financial sustainability
We find new revenue and fund development opportunities through environmental scans and market reports.
Leading through change
We facilitate the conversations your team needs to have now, so it can adapt, solve problems creatively and plan for what's ahead.

Renewing Strategy

You have a strategic plan, but it doesn't drive anything.

Goals were set without community evidence, no one agreed on how to measure success, and the plan sits apart from the budget and the year's work plans. Staff can't say what you're trying to achieve this year, and the board can't tell whether it's working.

How we can help

Strategic planning
We build plans grounded in community evidence that your teams use to set priorities all year.
Stakeholder engagement
We map and analyze your stakeholders and plan how to engage them, so you can be confident your strategy will have the impact you want.
KPIs and impact measures
We help boards develop a set of measures they understand and review at every meeting as part of their fiduciary duty and decision-making.
Plan review and reset
We rework an existing plan into goals people are actively working toward.
Annual work planning
We build annual work plans tied directly to your strategic goals.

Renewing Leadership

Your team has taken the courses and attended the institutes, but something is still missing in how you work together.

Leadership styles have become rigid, ideas get shot down before they're developed, trust is low, and no one says what needs to be said. When people do speak up, it rarely goes well.

How we can help

Executive coaching and advice
We act as an outside sounding board and bring proven practices that make your leadership work easier and more effective.
Leadership team coaching, workshops and retreats
We work with your team, online or in person, so the hard conversations happen, decisions get made, and people leave knowing what they're doing next.
Psychological safety
We help you see what shuts people down and what to change in your meetings, whether they happen in a room or on a screen, so ideas, honest conversation and creative problem-solving become how you work.

Renewing Projects

Projects slip quietly until a deadline forces attention.

Work gets assigned without a sponsor, a plan or an agreed definition of success, and when it finally lands, no one goes back to ask what happened.

How we can help

Project rescue
We get a project back on track with a plan and a timeline people believe in and can run with.
Project management coaching
We coach your team to run projects with clear goals, roles, milestones and expected outcomes.
Post-project review
We work with you to look at what worked and what didn't, and make sure it shapes the next project.

Not sure which area fits your situation? Book a call and we'll work it out together.

Who we are

RenewalWorks is Babs Kelly and Kevin Stranack. Between us, we've led libraries, non-profits and international open access projects, much of it with teams working remotely.

Babs Kelly

Babs Kelly (MLIS, MBA) has been a coach and facilitator for over 30 years along with a career in library management and as a non-profit executive director. For the past 10 years she has been a much sought after and trusted consultant with a focus on organizational strategy, governance, and leadership development. She listens to understand, makes room for big conversations that lead to action, and holds herself and others to a high standard of ethics and accountability.

How we work

We respect the work your organization does and the communities it serves. Every engagement is confidential, and we pay close attention to detail and to people.

We design processes and facilitate conversations that fit your situation and rest on evidence. You work with the two of us directly, as colleagues you can know and trust.

We work online and in person. Both of us have spent years leading and facilitating remote teams, including Kevin's time leading PKP's distributed international team and Babs' many years designing and facilitating online workshops, coaching, and stakeholder engagement. Our online sessions get the same design and care as in-person ones, and they make it easier to include staff, board and community members who can't travel. That matters for organizations spread across large regions, like many of the organizations we've worked with.

We value:

  • Respect, kindness, and friendliness

  • Openness, clarity, transparency, and accountability

  • Evidence, sense-making, and answering the “so what?”
