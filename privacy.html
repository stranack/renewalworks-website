Privacy

This page covers what we collect through renewalworks.ca, why we have it, and how to ask us to get rid of it.

RenewalWorks Consulting is a two-person firm based in Regina, Saskatchewan. We are not in the data business, and nothing we collect here is sold, rented, or passed to anyone for marketing.

When you send us a message

The form on our contact section asks for your name, email address, organization and a short description of what's going on. That message goes to Formspree (opens in a new tab), a form-handling service, which passes it on to us by email and keeps a copy in our account there. We read it, we reply, and we keep the correspondence while we are talking and for as long as we have an ongoing relationship with you or your organization. If you would rather skip the form, email us directly at hello@renewalworks.ca.

Anything you tell us about your organization's situation stays between us. Every engagement we take on is confidential, and enquiries are treated the same way.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to see which parts of the site people actually read and roughly where visitors come from. It sets cookies in your browser and collects the pages you view, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and details about your browser and device. We look at this in aggregate. It does not tell us who you are, and we do not try to connect it to anyone who contacts us.

If you would prefer not to be counted, browser settings that block cookies or trackers will stop it, as will Google's own opt-out add-on (opens in a new tab).

Fonts and hosting

The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when a page loads. The site itself is hosted by Bluehost, whose servers keep standard access logs.

Asking us to delete something

Write to hello@renewalworks.ca and tell us what you want removed. We will delete your enquiry and our copies of it, and confirm when it's done. You can also ask us what we hold about you, and we'll tell you.

Changes

If we change how any of this works, we'll update this page and change the date below.
